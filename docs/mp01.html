After completing the EDA pass using the glimpse() and skim() I can say that the cleaned up dataset consists of 61892 rows and has a 11 columns total. Each row has a unique category, such as year, period (period start/end), title, runtime, views, hours viewed, release date, global availability, and whether or not the content was a live event or pre-taped. All in all, the data pipeline for the Netlfix report was completed successfully.

Exploratory Questions & Summary Findings

An analysis of the Netflix engagement data reveals several key behavioral patterns across the catalog: * Coverage Window: The dataset spans from 2023-01-01 to 2026-06-30, encompassing 24,427 unique catalog titles. * Franchise Impact: High-profile intellectual property drives massive recurring engagement, with the Bridgerton universe pulling in 4,862,800,000 hours and the five main seasons of Stranger Things generating 5,588,900,000 hours of watch time. * Top-Tier Concentration: The Top 10 titles alone accounted for 6.70% of all watch time in 2026H1. * Global Reach: Approximately 35.1% of titles are available globally, which expands to 51.9% when weighted by total hours viewed.
